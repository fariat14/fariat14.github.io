About

I start with the question behind the numbers.

At Johnson Controls, I translate finance requirements into data definitions, validate information across enterprise systems, and help teams trust what they report.

Before this role, I worked in pricing analytics, executive reporting, market research, and graduate teaching. Economics taught me to question assumptions; those roles taught me to explain findings to different audiences.

Outside my day job, I build public dashboards and models, write about economics and ideas, and share what I learn with students and early-career professionals.

One of 100 young delegates representing Bangladesh

I was selected from thousands of applicants through a two-round process for one of 100 places in the prestigious Bangladesh Youth Delegation to India, an international exchange organized by the High Commission of India in Dhaka.
