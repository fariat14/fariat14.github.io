About

Business context meets technical depth.

I'm a data and analytics professional at Johnson Controls, where I support finance transformation through source-to-target mapping, SQL validation, and collaboration across finance and technology teams.

My earlier work spans pricing analytics, executive reporting, graduate teaching, and applied economics research. I bring those perspectives together to make data useful to the people making decisions.
