Profile

About Me

My career has moved from financial analysis to enterprise data analytics. I now map ERP finance data into Snowflake, validate transformations, and work with finance and engineering teams to resolve data issues.

At Johnson Controls, I have worked across State Tax, Treasury, Controllership, Pricing, and field business FP&A. I supported full P&L analysis for the approximately $850 million BSNA Canada region and its 2,500 employees. The company reported $23.6 billion in fiscal 2025 sales.

Earlier, I worked in consulting and market and social research. I also taught statistical modeling, business analytics methods, and R programming to more than 450 students at the University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee.

I hold an MBA in Business Analytics from UW–Milwaukee and a BSS in Economics from the University of Dhaka.
